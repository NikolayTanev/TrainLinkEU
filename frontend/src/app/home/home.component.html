HOW TRAINLINK WORKS

Three simple steps to organize your home workout routine

1

SAVE YOUR FAVORITE WORKOUTS

Browse YouTube and save workouts from your favorite trainers. Copy any YouTube URL and paste it into TrainLink - we'll automatically grab the video details, duration, and thumbnail. Build your personal library of go-to home workouts.

2

SCHEDULE YOUR WEEK

Plan your home workouts in advance using our calendar view. Drag and drop your saved workouts to specific days and times. Set up recurring schedules like "Yoga Mondays" or "HIIT Fridays" to build consistent habits.

WORKOUT & TRACK PROGRESS

When it's time to workout, just click and go! TrainLink opens your scheduled YouTube video so you can start immediately. Mark workouts as complete to track your consistency and build that satisfying streak of home fitness success.
